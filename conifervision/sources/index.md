# Literature sources

One OKF concept per ingested paper (`type: Source`). Raw PDFs live under `raw/papers/` (repo root, often gitignored).

Prioritized reading order for dense ITD: [Literature map — dense ITD](/concepts/literature-map-dense-itd.md).

## Ingested papers

* [Wang et al. (2013) — Crown and root biomass equations for the small trees of Pinus koraiensis under canopy](/sources/paper-crown_and_root_biomass_equations_fo.md) — demonstrates crown width utility over height and competition suppression for understory Pinus koraiensis allometry
* [Contrasted allometries between stem diameter, crown area, and tree height in five tropical biogeographic areas](/sources/paper-blanchard_tsaf_2016.md) — Multi-site tropical empirical study showing DBH-crown area scaling exponents remain invariant (~1.24) across sites, but height scaling and crown expansion vary with canopy emergence.
* [Biomass and stem volume equations for tree species in Europe](/sources/paper-biomass%20and%20stem%20volume_951-40-1984-9.md) — synthesis of 607 biomass and 230 stem volume equations across 55 European tree species with transformation corrections
* [Optimising profits from timber harvest and the biodiversity conservation value in a central European beech forest using a novel bioeconomic forestry model (Supplementary Material)](/sources/paper-beech%20allometry_bgc4810s1.md) — Empirical double-logistic crown area and modified Mund height-to-DBH allometric equations for European beech, hornbeam, lime, and maple.
* [Allometric Relationships of Selected European Tree Species](/sources/paper-allometric%20relationships%20of%20selected%20european%20tree-gp_eudor_pdfa1b_euna20855enc_001.md) — synthesizes empirical allometric equations (DBH, height, crown radius, biomass) and crown geometric primitives for spruce, pine, birch, beech, and larch to constrain crown delineation and volume inversion
* [Stem volume equations and basic density for grey alder and common alder in Sweden](/sources/paper-alder_allometry_cpi023.md) — Stem volume equations, wood basic density, and crown base height taper relationships for Alnus incana and Alnus glutinosa in Sweden.
* [A Linkage among Tree Diameter, Height, Crown Base Height, and Crown Width 4-Variate Distribution and Their Growth Models: A 4-Variate Diffusion Process Approach](/sources/paper-a_linkage_among_tree_diameter_height_crown_base_he.md) — Vasicek-type 4-variate SDE joint distribution showing that conditioning on both CHM height and crown width predicts DBH ($R^2 \approx 0.89$) and stem volume without below-canopy metrics
* [A rapid method for estimating the median diameter of the stem profile of Norway spruce (Picea abies Karst) trees](/sources/paper-a%20rapid%20method%20for%20estimating%20the%20median%20diameter%20of%20the%20stem%20profile%20of%20norway%20spruce%20trees-iforest_ifor1945-009_vasilescu.md) — empirical stem profile models locating median diameter at 0.3H for Norway spruce stem volume and taper estimation
* [A Crown Width-Diameter Model for Natural Even-Aged Black Pine Forest Management](/sources/paper-a%20crown%20width-diameter%20model%20for%20natural%20even-aged%20black%20pine%20forest%20management-forests-09-00610-v2.md) — nonlinear mixed-effects models relating horizontal crown width to DBH and stand density covariates with plot-level calibration
* [Site Index Models for Main Forest-Forming Tree Species in Poland](/sources/paper-site%20index%20models%20for%20main%20forest-forming%20tree%20species%20in%20poland_forests-11-00301.md) — GADA-based dynamic height growth and site index equations for eight Central European tree species fitted on 3,052 stem analyses, superseding historical yield tables
* [Mean height or dominant height – what to prefer for modelling the site index of Estonian forests?](/sources/paper-mean_height_or_dominant_height_–_what_to_prefer_for_modelling_the_site_index_of_estonian_forests.md) — demonstrates dominant height insensitivity to low thinning and provides dominant-to-mean height regression for Estonian forests
* [Extraction of Individual Tree Attributes Using Ultra-High-Density Point Clouds Acquired by Low-Cost UAV-LiDAR in Eucalyptus Plantations](/sources/paper-extraction_of_individual_tree_attributes_using_ultra-high-density_point_clouds_acquired_by_low-cost%20uav-lidar_in_eucalyptus_plantations.md) — bottom-up stem clustering with HDBSCAN, chi-square radial distribution DBH modeling, and pseudo-waveform tree height filtering from ultra-dense low-cost UAV-LiDAR
* [Evaluation of growth models used in the Swedish Forest Planning System Heureka](/sources/paper-evaluation%20of%20growth%20functions%20used%20in%20the%20swedish%20forest%20planning%20system%20heureka_article1013.md) — Stand vs single-tree empirical basal area and volume growth evaluation in Sweden (Heureka system)
* [Mapping site index in coniferous forests using bi-temporal airborne laser scanning data and field data from the Swedish national forest inventory](/sources/paper-appiahmensah_et_al_2023_site_index_sis_rs.md) — Bi-temporal ALS height percentiles, annual top-height increment, and terrain auxiliaries predicting coniferous site index across Sweden via multiple linear regression
* [Modelling potential yield capacity in conifers using Swedish long-term experiments](/sources/paper-appiahmensah_et%20_al-2022_yield%20capacity.md) — Yield capacity and stand volume models connecting CHM top height, basal area, and species to timber yield across Swedish conifer trials
* [RefDrone: A Challenging Benchmark for Referring Expression Comprehension in Drone Scenes](/sources/paper-refdrone_llms.md) — introduces RefDrone benchmark and Number GroundingDINO (NGDINO) for multi-target and no-target aerial referring expression comprehension
* [Multiple Large AI Models' Consensus for Object Detection—A Survey](/sources/paper-multi_llm_detections.md) — Iwanowski (2025) — Survey of multi-LLM/VLM consensus frameworks for object detection, categorizing prompt-level, reasoning, box-level, and hybrid consensus.
* [INSID3: Training-Free In-Context Segmentation with DINOv3](/sources/paper-insid3_in_context_segmentation_dinov3.md) — INSID3 — training-free in-context segmentation using SVD-debiased DINOv3 features
* [Zero-Shot Tree Detection and Segmentation from Aerial Forest Imagery](/sources/paper-zero-shot_tree_detection_and_segmentation_from_aerial_forest_imagery-2506-03114v1.md) — Chen (2025) — Evaluates SAM2 zero-shot tree crown segmentation prompted by DeepForest bounding boxes.
* [UAV-Based LiDAR Scanning for Individual Tree Detection and Height Measurement in Young Forest Permanent Trials](/sources/paper-uav-based_lidar_scanning_for_individual_tree_detection_and_height_measurement_in_young_forest-5_2021.md) — Rodríguez-Puerta (2022) — Evaluates ITD and height extraction on young pine plantations, finding 10 cm CHM optimal and point cloud height extraction unbiased.
* [TreePseCo: Scaling Individual Tree Crown Segmentation using Large Vision Models](/sources/paper-treepseco_scaling_individual_tree_crown_segmentation_using_large_vision_models-isprs-archives-xlviii-m-7-2025-275-2025.md) — TreePseCo — adapts PseCo framework using SAM and Faster R-CNN for robust individual tree crown segmentation in aerial imagery
* [Tree crown segmentation in three dimensions using density models derived from airborne laser scanning](/sources/paper-tree_crown_segmentation_in_three_dimensions_using_density_models_derived_from_airborne_laser_scanning-holmgren_j_et_al_220114.md) — Holmgren et al. (2022) — Introduces 3D tree crown density models and mean-shift clustering to detect understory trees in multi-layered forest canopies.
* [The accuracy of image-based individual tree crown detection and delineation across vegetation types](/sources/paper-the_accuracy_of_image-based_individual_tree_crown_detection_and_delineation_across_vegetation_types-isprs-archives-xlviii-g-2025-1223-2025.md) — Pucino (2025) — Evaluates combinations of 3 CHM and 4 ITS algorithms across 15 Australian vegetation classes, finding Pit-Free CHM and DalPonte segmentation yield the highest accuracy.
* [Self-Supervised Learning for Precise Individual Tree Segmentation in Airborne LiDAR Point Clouds](/sources/paper-self-supervised_learning_for_precise_individual_tree_segmentation_in_airborne_lidar_point_clouds.md) — Shaheen (2025) — Self-supervised 3D LiDAR point cloud segmentation using transformation-invariant pretext tasks and energy-based soft clustering.
* [Optimizing aerial imagery collection and processing parameters for drone-based individual tree mapping in structurally complex conifer forests](/sources/paper-optimizing_aerial_imagery_collection_and_processing_parameters_for_drone-based_individual_tree_mapping_in_structurally_complex_conifer_forests.md) — Young et al. (2022) — Comprehensive evaluation of 7,568 combinations of drone flight parameters, photogrammetry settings, and ITD algorithms in a complex mixed-conifer stand.
* [LiDAR Point Cloud Denoising for Individual Tree Extraction Based on the Noise4Denoise](/sources/paper-lidar_point_cloud_denoising_for_individual_tree_extraction_based_on_the_noise4denoise-fpls-15-1490660.md) — Lu (2025) — Introduces DEN4, an unsupervised deep learning-based point cloud denoising algorithm that improves individual tree segmentation accuracy.
* [Individual Tree Segmentation Based on Region-Growing and Density-Guided Canopy 3-D Morphology Detection Using UAV LiDAR Data](/sources/paper-individual_tree_segmentation_based_on_region-growing_and_density-guided_canopy_3-d_morphology_detection_using_uav_lidar_data.md) — Li (2025) — introduces RDT, a hybrid tree segmentation framework combining CHM region-growing with point cloud density-guided vertical profile morphology analysis
* [Individual tree crown delineation in high resolution aerial RGB imagery using StarDist-based model](/sources/paper-individual_tree_crown_delineation_in_high_resolution_aerial_rgb_imagery_using_stardist-based_model-1-s2-0-s0034425725000227-main.md) — Tong (2025) — Adapts StarDist with a sparse-annotation loss function for individual tree crown delineation in dense RGB imagery.
* [ForestFormer3D: A Unified Framework for End-to-End Segmentation of Forest LiDAR 3D Point Clouds](/sources/paper-forestformer3d_a_unified_framework_for_end-to-end_segmentation_of_forest_lidar_3dpoint_clouds-2506-16991v1.md) — ForestFormer3D — End-to-end transformer-based 3D instance and semantic segmentation framework for forest LiDAR point clouds using ISA-guided query selection and score-based block merging.
* [Cross-Comparison of Individual Tree Detection Methods Using Low and High Pulse Density Airborne Laser Scanning Data](/sources/paper-cross-comparision-of-itd-methods-using-low-and-hight-pulse-density-als-2022-sparks-et-al.md) — Sparks (2022) — Comparative study of seven ITD methods across mixed-conifer stands using 8 and 22 ppm² ALS data, showing that stand density and parameter tuning drive accuracy more than method choice.
* [Comparison of individual tree detection methods](/sources/paper-comparison_of_individual_tree-twec21_public.md) — Weckman (2021) — Comparative study of local maxima, trunk detection, and point density methods for individual tree detection using high-density UAV laser scanning.
* [Canopy LiDAR Point Cloud Data K-Means Clustering Watershed Segmentation Method](/sources/paper-canopy_lidar_point_cloud_data_k-means_clustering_watershed_segmentation_method-isprs-annals-vi-3-w1-2020-67-2020.md) — Mu (2020) — Canopy Maximum Model (CMM) and seeded K-means watershed segmentation to mitigate over-segmentation.
* [BorFIT: A Novel LiDAR-Based Training Dataset for Individual Tree Segmentation and Species Detection in northern boreal Forests](/sources/paper-borfit_a_novel_lidar-based_training_dataset_for_individual_tree-essd-2025-340.md) — BorFIT — A manually segmented and species-classified boreal forest LiDAR dataset with structural and spectral metrics
* [A simple oriented search and clustering method for extracting individual forest trees from ALS point clouds](/sources/paper-a%20simple%20oriented%20search%20and%20clustering%20method%20for%20extracting%20individual%20forest%20trees%20from%20als%20point%20clouds-1-s2-0-s157495412400520x-main.md) — Ding (2025) — bottom-up oriented search and clustering method for extracting individual trees from ALS point clouds
* [LGINet: Linguistic Guided Image Diffusion Model for Tree Species Generation and Identification](/sources/paper-linguistic_guided_image_diffusion_model_for_tree_species_generation.md) — Yun (2026) — LGINet text-guided diffusion model for synthetic tree canopy generation and enhanced YOLOv11 species identification
* [TreeFlow: Conditional Flow Matching for 3D Tree Point Cloud Generation from Inventory Attributes](/sources/paper-treeflow.md) — TreeFlow — Conditional Flow Matching for 3D tree point cloud generation from inventory attributes
* [FSOD-VFM: Few-Shot Object Detection with Vision Foundation Models and Graph Diffusion](/sources/paper-fsod-vfm.md) — FSOD-VFM — A training-free few-shot object detection framework using UPN, SAM2, and DINOv2/v3 with graph diffusion to mitigate proposal overfragmentation.
* [A Closer Look at Cross-Domain Few-Shot Object Detection: Fine-Tuning Matters and Parallel Decoder Helps](/sources/paper-fine-tuning_matters_and_parallel_decoder_helps.md) — Yu et al. (2026) — Introduces Hybrid Ensemble Decoder (HED) and progressive fine-tuning to stabilize few-shot object detection and reduce OOD overconfidence.
* [EdgeCrafter: Compact ViTs for Edge Dense Prediction via Task-Specialized Distillation](/sources/paper-edgecrafter.md) — EdgeCrafter — compact ViT framework utilizing task-specialized DINOv3 distillation for edge dense prediction
* [Real-Time Object Detection Meets DINOv3](/sources/paper-deimv2.md) — DEIMv2 — Real-time DETR-based detector integrating DINOv3 backbones via Spatial Tuning Adapters
* [Popescu & Wynne (2004) — LiDAR, multispectral fusion, CHM, local maxima](/sources/popescu-wynne-2004-seeing-the-trees.md) — LiDAR CHM, variable-window local maxima, multispectral fusion (2004)
* [Miao et al. (2024) — Pseudo tree crown (PTC) for UAV individual tree species classification](/sources/miao-zhang-2024-ptc-uav-species.md) — pseudo tree crown (PTC), UAV species classification, ResNet50/PyTorch (2024)
* [Automatic Data Curation for Self-Supervised Learning: A Clustering-Based Approach](/sources/vo-2024-automatic-data-curation.md) — hierarchical k-means SSL data curation, satellite canopy height (Meta FAIR, 2024)
